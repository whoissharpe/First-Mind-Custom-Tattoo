The studio

Family owned, and it shows.

FIRSTMIND is owned and run by Natalie and Alex Braddock. It’s a small, family-operated shop — which means you deal with the people who actually do the work, from the first message to the last line.

Single-use needles, hospital-grade sterilisation, and a room that’s kept clean because we work in it every day. Bring your idea, your reference photos, or nothing but a rough thought — we’ll draw from there.

What we do

Styles

Not sure which one fits your idea? That’s what the consultation is for.

  • Neo-traditional

    Bold outlines, rich colour, ornamental detail. Built to stay readable as it ages.

  • Black & grey

    Smooth greywash shading and contrast — portraits, nature, script, realism.

  • Fine line

    Delicate single-needle linework for botanicals, lettering and small pieces.

  • American traditional

    Heavy black, solid fill, classic subject matter. The stuff that lasts fifty years.

  • Cover-ups & reworks

    Honest assessment first. If it can be saved, we’ll tell you how — and if it can’t, we’ll tell you that too.

  • Custom flash

    Hand-drawn sheets ready to go, on the wall and on our socials.

How it works

From idea to healed

  1. 01

    Get in touch

    Call, email, or message us on Instagram or Facebook with your idea, rough size, and where on the body it’s going.

  2. 02

    Consultation

    We talk through placement, scale and budget in person or over the phone, then quote you honestly.

  3. 03

    The drawing

    Your design gets drawn custom. You see it before the machine ever comes out, and changes are part of the deal.

  4. 04

    Tattoo day

    Eat beforehand, wear something comfortable, bring headphones. We’ll take the breaks you need.

  5. 05

    Healing

    You leave with clear aftercare instructions and a number to call if anything looks off.
